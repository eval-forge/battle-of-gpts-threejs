One prompt.
Seven horizons.

Each test was asked to build the same single-file Three.js experience: a floating island called “Lighthouse Cove,” complete with a lighthouse beam, tiny village, boats, islets, waterfalls, seagulls, procedural helpers, and day/night interaction.

THE CHALLENGE
Build a beautiful, interactive 3D voxel scene from scratch. Make it dense, polished, smooth at 60fps, and complete enough to download and open as one HTML file.
