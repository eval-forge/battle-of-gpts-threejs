VIEW FULL LIGHTHOUSE COVE PROMPT
Build a single-file HTML page using Three.js (load from cdnjs) that renders an interactive 3D voxel scene: “Lighthouse Cove”.

Scene:
• A floating island with sandy beaches on one side, grassy cliffs on the other, and a jagged rocky underside with waterfalls spilling off the edge into the void.
• A tall striped lighthouse on the cliff with a rotating beam of light.
• A tiny fishing village: 4 to 5 colorful cottages, a wooden dock, two little sailboats bobbing in a voxel water pool.
• Palm trees, bushes, flower patches, barrels, crates, a campfire with animated flickering.
• 3 small floating islets nearby, each with its own mini scene (one with a lone tree, one with a stack of treasure chests).
• Seagulls (simple voxel birds) circling the lighthouse.

Visuals:
• Sunset sky gradient, blocky clouds drifting, warm directional light with soft shadows, light fog.
• InstancedMesh for performance, subtle per-voxel color variation so nothing looks flat.
• Water with gentle animated color shimmer.

Interaction:
• Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, auto-rotate when idle.
• UI overlay with a title card top left and buttons for day/night, auto-rotate, and a toggle for the lighthouse beam.
• Night mode: starry sky, glowing windows, the lighthouse beam becomes the main light source.

Generate everything procedurally with helper functions (addCottage(), addPalmTree(), addBoat()). Make it dense, polished, and smooth at 60fps. Output the complete working code.
